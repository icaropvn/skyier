**SKYIER** is my very first video game. It was originally created as part of a course in my Software Engineering degree, but I continued putting time and effort into improving it even after the assignment was completed.

Inspired by *Gato Roboto* and *Celeste*, **SKYIER** is a 2D pixel-art platformer where your goal is to climb your way to the top of each level. Along the way, you'll face dangerous gaps and deadly spikes, while discovering upgrades that will help you overcome the challenges ahead.

This project was my first hands-on experience with game development and the Unity Engine. I learned a lot throughout the process and finally got a better understanding of what it actually takes to develop a game. Despite its simplicity, I'm really happy with what I've created, and I hope to build on this experience to make even cooler things in the future!
